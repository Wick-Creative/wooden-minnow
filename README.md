# Wooden Minnow

Hollow Lake on your taskbar: a tiny always-on-top window that plays the lake while you work. It is
the Wooden Minnow keychain's own screen, on your desktop.

## Download

Get the newest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/Wick-Creative/wooden-minnow/releases/latest)**:

- **Windows:** `Wooden-Minnow-Setup-<version>.exe`
- **Mac:** `Wooden-Minnow-<version>-mac.dmg` (Intel and Apple silicon)

These builds are not signed yet, so your computer will warn you the first time:

- **Windows:** SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** open the `.dmg` and drag Wooden Minnow to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS
  says it cannot check it. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
  **Open Anyway**, then open it again.

## Playing

The first screen asks whether you are new (pick a name) or already fish at Hollow Lake (it shows a
code; text `MINNOW` and the code to the lake from your phone, and it plays your game).

- Arrow keys or A / D, Space, Backspace, M for the menu, T to type.
- Hover over it for the grip (move), the menu (size, the screen while you wait, start at login) and
  close. Right-click or the tray icon opens the same menu.

## Updates

On Windows it updates itself: a new version downloads quietly and installs when the Minnow closes.
On a Mac it tells you when there is a new version and links here.
